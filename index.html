Inquire within, upon everything!

Hi! My name is Vaibhav and I was born and raised in a small hill-station named Nainital. This online space is representative of a personal diary where I can share my thoughts and have them reviewed, all the while observe them evolve overtime myself. If you've visited "theboxtroll.com" before, then the changes you now see are due to the fact that I have now switched to GitHub Pages from BlueHost to avoid the hosting costs. I am yet to transfer all the posts and content, but hopefully it would be done soon.

I also maintain another blog called Know Thyself. The idea here is to summarize the main concepts from some of my favorite non-fiction books that have been important in shaping my world model. I try to write these summaries in a way that would be accessible to my 7-year-old cousin (as referred to here), but I believe they could be just as useful to adults alike!
